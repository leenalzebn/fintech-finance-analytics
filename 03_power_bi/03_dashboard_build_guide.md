# Power BI Dashboard Build Guide

## Page 1 — Finance Executive Overview
KPI cards: Payment Volume EUR, Fee Revenue EUR, Settlement Rate %, Failure Rate %, Reconciliation Exceptions.
Visuals: monthly payment-volume trend, fee revenue by plan, volume by country, MoM growth. Slicers: Date, Country, Plan.

## Page 2 — Reconciliation & Data Quality
KPI cards: Reconciliation Variance EUR, Reconciliation Exceptions, Settled Transactions.
Visuals: daily variance, exception table, exception count by channel, quality-control summary.

## Page 3 — Payments & Revenue
Visuals: volume by transaction type, failure rate by channel, failure reason analysis, fee revenue by plan, merchant-category performance.

## Page 4 — Risk & Regulatory View
Visuals: high-risk volume share by country, active customers by risk rating, high-risk customer table, chargeback rate by merchant category, chargeback amount trend.
